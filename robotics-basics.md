# Robotics Basics

Robotics is a field that combines mechanical engineering, electronics, programming, sensors, and automation to create machines that can perform specific tasks.

## What Is Robotics?

A robot is a programmable machine designed to carry out tasks automatically or with human control. Robots can be used in education, manufacturing, healthcare, agriculture, logistics, research, and many other areas.

## Basic Components of a Robot

- Sensors
- Motors
- Microcontrollers
- Power supply
- Mechanical structure
- Programming software

## How Robots Work

Sensors collect information from the environment. A controller processes that information according to programmed instructions, and motors or other actuators perform the required action.

## Why Learn Robotics?

Learning robotics can help students develop programming, logical thinking, creativity, problem-solving, and engineering skills.

A strong robotics training program should combine theoretical concepts with practical projects so learners can understand how individual components work together.
